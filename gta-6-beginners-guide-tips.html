GTA 6 Beginner's Guide: Essential Tips for Your First Hours

The systems that trip up new players in Leonida — saving, protecting your cash, Focus, training, the weapons you keep, and how to stay off the police radar. Every tip here is stated by at least two released-game guides; sources are listed at the end.

1. Save manually — missions can't be replayed

Open the pause menu and use Game → Save Game. Completed story missions can't be replayed, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter in case you want to go back.

2. Protect your cash

Cash you're carrying is lost if you die or get busted. Until you can bank it, hide it in the stash under the floor of Jason's bedroom. A bank account opens with the Buck Me mission partway through Chapter 1 — after that, deposit regularly.

3. Weapons you buy are yours; pickups aren't

Guns bought from a store stay with you after death. Guns you pick up are lost — unless you store them in your personal car's trunk, which makes them yours. The game starts in Key Lento, and its Ammu-Nation (Chuck's Tackle & Shot) is open right after the opening section.

4. Use Focus constantly

  • Activate: click both sticks (L3 + R3).
  • What it shows: cash, loot and things you can interact with.
  • Train it: fishing and shooting hoops raise Focus. Feeding Jason's pet snake, Dolores, gives a temporary boost.

5. Train at Jason's house

Lifting weights raises maximum health; cardio raises stamina. A protein shake from the fridge helps after a workout. Sleeping in Jason's bed advances time.

6. Stay off the police radar

Police can recognise your outfit, face, car, partner and weapons — shown as icons under the wanted stars. Holster your gun in public, keep a spare outfit in your trunk, and destroy CCTV footage in a store's back room after a robbery. Your Criminal Profile runs across five tiers from Professional to Irredeemable: killing pushes you down, while non-lethal play keeps you at Professional, where non-lethal takedowns also refill Focus.

7. Handy tools and an early bonus

  • Wrecked your car? Call Joey to have it repaired and delivered.
  • WAiNK app: shows a car's sell value and registration cost.
  • Simplified Mini Games setting makes the gym, basketball and hotwiring easier.
  • Shirley's rum (Chapter 1): follow the parrot's clue to cash in a wall vent by a yellow generator behind the liquor store. The errand is missable.

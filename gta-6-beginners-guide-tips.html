GTA 6 Beginner's Guide: 15 Things You Must Know First

Welcome to the state of Leonida. Rockstar has overhauled core systems: inventory wheels are gone, civilians film crimes with phone cameras, cars have limited trunk storage, and flash floods change getaways dynamically. Here is your Day-1 survival playbook.

Stepping into Vice City for the first time is intoxicating, but treating GTA 6 like GTA 5 will get you arrested, killed, or broke within your first two hours. Rockstar Games has layered realism into physics, law enforcement, inventory management, and economic systems.

1. The Car Trunk is Your Heavy Armory

The days of pulling rocket launchers, sniper rifles, miniguns, and assault rifles out of your back pocket are over. GTA 6 adopts a grounded inventory system: you can carry two concealed handguns, a melee weapon, and up to two shoulder-slung long guns.

Everything else — heavy assault rifles, shotguns, sniper bags, and body armor spares — must be stored in the trunk of your vehicle. When pulling up to a robbery or gunfight, you must physically walk to your trunk and equip your loadout before breaching.

Vice Vault Tactical Tip

Never park your primary vehicle more than 30 meters from an objective. If local police shoot out your tires or box your car in, you lose access to your heavy weapons.

2. Civilians Pull Out Phone Cameras First

Witness AI in Leonida is hyper-realistic. When gunfire erupts, pedestrians don't merely run away screaming; bystanders frequently take out smartphones to record you, snap license plate numbers, or live-stream your getaway.

If a pedestrian manages to upload footage or place a 911 call, police receive an accurate description of your face, clothing, and vehicle model. You can stop 911 calls by aiming at civilians to intimidate them, smashing phones, or ditching the immediate 3-block radius before the call connects.

3. Switch Vehicles Immediately After Any Felony

Police dispatchers track vehicle license plates and paint jobs. If you escape a 2-star or higher wanted level in a stolen red Bravado Gauntlet, cops across that district remain on high alert for that specific car.

Crucial Heat Warning

Cruising past a police cruiser in the vehicle you committed a robbery in will instantly re-trigger your wanted level, even if the stars have faded. Ditch your ride in an alley and hijack or purchase an untainted car.

4. Dual Protagonists Have Distinct Tactical Perks

Jason and Lucia are not simple cosmetic swaps; each possesses distinct mechanical bonuses:

  • Lucia (The Tactician): Quicker lockpicking speed, faster zip-tying of hostages during robberies, and higher stealth takedown radius in indoor spaces.
  • Jason (The Muscle & Driver): Slower stamina depletion during sprint, higher recoil stabilization with heavy firearms, and superior recovery when vehicles hydroplane.

5. Never Carry Over $5,000 Cash on Hand

Leonida's streets are dangerous. If you are busted or wasted while carrying large amounts of cash, you forfeit a percentage of your liquid funds. Furthermore, muggers and opportunistic NPCs can target you in sketchy back-alleys.

Always use the in-game Maze Bank Mobile App on your smartphone to wire cash deposits into your checking account before undertaking risky missions or heists.

6. Dynamic Hurricane Weather Floods Coastal Streets

The state of Leonida features full dynamic tropical storm systems. When severe weather hits, coastal areas around Vice Beach and Ocean Drive experience flash flooding and high tides.

Low-riding sports cars will stall out or hydroplane violently in standing water. When storm sirens wail, switch to lifted pickup trucks, SUVs, or off-road buggies to navigate flooded streets.

7. Weapon Cleaning & Swamp Rust Degrade Performance

Dragging your firearms through the brackish water and mud of the Grasslands or Gator Keys causes weapons to foul. A fouled gun suffers from slower reload times, reduced bullet damage, and occasional trigger jams during firefights.

Purchase gun oil kits from any Ammu-Nation or gunsmith to clean your arsenal at your safehouse workbench.

8. Pawn Shops & Local Fences Pay 85% Value

Stealing high-end luxury goods (Rolex watches, designer bags, gold necklaces) from luxury homes and hotels yields great money, but regular stores will not touch them. Locate underground fences in Little Haiti and Port Gellhorn to cash in contraband without police tracing.

9. Hair & Beard Growth Are Linked to In-Game Days

Just like Red Dead Redemption 2, hair and facial hair grow organically over in-game days. If you want a specific fade, pompadour, or handlebar mustache, visit local barbers in Vice City regularly.

10. Gator Hazards in the Mangroves Are Lethal

The swamps of the Grasslands are inhabited by apex predators. American alligators in GTA 6 will stalk players who wade through waist-deep waters, performing sudden death-roll attacks that deal fatal damage. Stick to fan-boats and elevated boardwalks.

11. DualSense Adaptive Trigger Aiming (PS5)

On PlayStation 5, the DualSense controller uses two-stage trigger resistance. A half-pull on L2 engages quick-draw hip-fire focus, while a full trigger depression locks your iron-sights for pinpoint headshots. Practice this in the Ammu-Nation shooting range.

12. Intimidate Store Clerks to Avoid Silent Alarm Buttons

When holding up convenience stores, check the counter carefully. Many cashiers will slowly reach beneath the register to hit a silent silent alarm wired to local police. Firing a warning shot into the ceiling or liquor bottles behind them prevents them from triggering the alarm.

13. Don't Waste Early Cash on Supercars

The fastest way to stunt your early progression is spending your initial $250,000 on a supercar that will inevitably get wrecked or seized by police. Instead, invest in:

  • Silencers and extended magazines for your sidearms.
  • Electronic lockpick bypass tools for hotel doors and ATM skimmers.
  • A reinforced safehouse stash for holding jewelry and contraband.

14. Check In-Game Social Media for High-Value Leads

Vice Vault intel shows that the in-game social media app (WhatUp / Vice Clips) contains real-time tips. NPCs frequently post photos boasting of exotic cars parked at Starfish Island or yacht parties carrying loose diamonds.

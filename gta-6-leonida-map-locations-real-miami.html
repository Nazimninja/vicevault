GTA 6 Leonida Map Locations vs Real-World Florida Comparison

Explore the GTA 6 Leonida map. A side-by-side analysis of leaks comparing Vice City to Miami, the Gator Keys to Florida Keys, and Grasslands to the Everglades.

Mapping the State of Leonida to Real Florida

Rockstar is scaling up from a single city to an entire mock state. The state of Leonida is a highly detailed, satirical recreation of Florida, featuring urban centers, swamp networks, beach communities, and islands.

Community mapping projects analyzing leaked coordinates show that Leonida's landmass is roughly 2.5 times larger than GTA 5's Los Santos, providing vast areas for exploration and dynamic gameplay.

Major Vice City Zones & Real counterparts

Here are the verified areas mapped to real-world locations:

  • Vice City Mainland: Represents Miami, complete with a financial district (Brickell), a tourist strip (Ocean Drive), and suburbs resembling Little Havana.
  • Gator Keys: The GTA version of the Florida Keys. Expect long highway bridges connecting small islands, stilt houses, and active maritime traffic.
  • The Grasslands: Mapped to the Florida Everglades, home to hovercrafts, alligator farms, and rural outlaws.
  • Port Gellhorn: Based on Fort Myers/Naples on the Gulf coast, presenting a industrial and suburban atmosphere.

Dynamic Landscaping & Interactive Environments

Leonida isn't just larger; it's denser. Malls, supermarkets, clubs, and motels are fully accessible, creating a living world where players can walk into hundreds of buildings seamlessly.

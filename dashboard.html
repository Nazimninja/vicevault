How GTA 6 cheats work
  1. Finish the main story first. Until the credits roll, every code is rejected. After that a Cheats tab appears in the Progress menu.
  2. Unlock each cheat's challenge (shown in that tab). Progress you already made counts.
  3. Enter it on the phone: press Up on the D-pad to take out the phone, hold L1 + R1, then press the D-pad sequence. Sequences are identical on PS5 and Xbox.
  4. Save before you cheat. An active cheat disables saving and Trophies/Achievements for the session. Reload your last save to go back to normal.
There are no phone-number cheats and no PC version yet. Codes marked “not found yet” haven't been publicly discovered — we add them as soon as they're confirmed by multiple sources.

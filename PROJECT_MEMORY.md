## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

**Vice Vault** is a high-octane gaming media hub, pre-launch waitlist, and intelligence directory built for Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6). It serves as a content brand, audience aggregator, and newsletter funnel.

- **Production URL**: `https://vicevault.gg`

## 2. Technical Stack & Key Pages

- **Framework**: Semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, responsive vanilla JS, Cloudflare Pages edge hosting.
- **Core Pages**:
  - `index.html`: Hero countdown timer, feature trailers, interactive feature grid, email waitlist capture.
  - `blog.html`: Intelligence leaks, confirmed features, timeline updates, and rumor debunking.
  - `admin.html`: Content management and waitlist analytics view.
  - `about.html`: Brand mission and editorial guidelines.
  - `404.html`: Custom neon-themed Vice City 404 error page.

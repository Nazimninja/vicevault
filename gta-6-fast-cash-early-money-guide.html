GTA 6 Early Money Guide: How to Make Money in Chapters 1 & 2

The early-game money methods that multiple released-game guides agree on, who buys what, and how to avoid losing what you earn. No inflated per-hour figures — payouts vary and are still being mapped, so we'll add numbers once sources agree.

By: Vice Vault
Updated: 20 Nov 2026
Read Time: 6 min read
Difficulty: Beginner

First: don't lose what you earn

Cash on hand is lost when you die or get busted. Use the stash under the floor of Jason's bedroom, then your BuckMe bank account once the Buck Me mission opens it in Chapter 1. Bank after every score.

1. Hit Illegal Operations (Chapter 1+)

Gang hideouts — including the Kingfisher operations out in the swamps — hold narcotics, cash and other loot. In Chapter 1, Brian and Donnie are your outlets for the drugs you take. Expect armed guards, and remember that dying there costs you your pocket cash.

2. Kenton's treasure dives (Key Lento)

Work for Kenton in Key Lento (the "Island Dreams" mission) and he'll buy valuables you recover from wrecks. It's low risk and a steady early earner.

3. Sell stolen cars to Moses (Chapter 2)

From Chapter 2, Moses in Port Gellhorn buys stolen cars. Use the WAiNK app to check a car's value before you take it in.

4. Easy early cash: Shirley's rum

During this Chapter 1 errand, follow the parrot's clue to cash in a wall vent by a yellow generator behind the liquor store. It's missable, so do it when it appears.

Still being confirmed

Early guides disagree on exact payouts, whether store robberies open in Chapter 1 or 2, whether you can sell cars before meeting Moses, and how much Chapter 1 cash carries into Chapter 2. One guide reports that only savings carry over — so banking early is the safe play either way.

Early Game Economic Overview

Money in GTA 6 is considerably tighter than previous titles. Hospital bills scale with your net worth, weapon ammo is expensive, vehicle impound fees sting, and dirty cash from street robberies cannot be deposited directly into legitimate bank accounts without fencing.

Below is our empirical testing table ranking the top early game revenue streams by payout per hour and police risk factor.

1. Port Gellhorn Exotic Vehicle Dock Exports

Starting in Chapter 2, you can meet with dock broker "Clyde" at the Port Gellhorn maritime container terminal. Clyde provides an encrypted text list of 5 luxury cars wanted for overseas shipment to South America.

Head to the Starfish Island wealthy enclave or Downtown Vice City hotel valet lots. Use Lucia to quietly jimmy the lock, disable the GPS tracker within 60 seconds using the mobile hacking mini-game, and drive directly to Clyde's shipping container.

Speed Secret

Avoid any crashes during transit. Clyde docks 10% off the payout for every dent or broken headlight. Drive along the elevated highway instead of through crowded urban intersections.

2. Starfish Island Luxury Hotel Room Heists

Vice City's oceanfront resorts are filled with high-rolling cartel bosses and crypto millionaires who leave loose jewelry, gold bullion, and cold wallets in hotel suites.

Bribe the front desk concierge or obtain a master keycard from the laundry room, then silently loot the suites on the 4th and 5th floors between 1:00 AM and 5:00 AM. Pawn the stolen jewels at the Little Haiti fence for immediate untraceable greenbacks.

3. Grasslands Alligator Skin & Python Fencing

If you want pure cash without dodging police helicopters, head inland into the swamps of the Grasslands. Using a high-caliber bolt-action rifle, hunt 3-star mature alligators and Burmese pythons.

Take perfect pelts to the roadside butcher and taxidermist near Ambrosia. A pristine alligator pelt sells for $850 each, and a 40-minute fan-boat hunting expedition routinely yields $40,000+ clean cash with zero police heat.

4. Vice Beach Convenience Store Chain Hustle

The fastest burst cash for a broke player is chaining 4 convenience stores along the southern coast of Vice Beach.

  1. Equip a mask and park a getaway motorcycle facing the exit.
  2. Enter, draw a suppressed firearm, and fire one shot into the liquor shelf behind the cashier to intimidate them.
  3. Do not wait for them to open the register; shout command buttons to speed up cash bagging.
  4. Ditch the area into the beach dunes where police cruisers cannot follow without getting stuck in deep sand.

5. ATM Skimmer Device Placement (Passive Income)

Once you connect with hacker contacts on the in-game Dark Web forum, you can purchase magnetic ATM skimmer bugs for $1,500 each. Plant these on busy ATMs near the Vice City Marina, Ocean Drive clubs, and the Downtown financial center.

Every 24 in-game hours, victims' cloned debit cards yield between $2,000 and $4,000 per skimmer, deposited directly into your crypto wallet without raising police heat.

3 Early Money Mistakes to Avoid

Fatal Financial Mistakes

1. Buying Luxury Cars First: Supercars depreciate rapidly and carry high repair costs. Spend early capital on silencers, body armor, lockpicks, and safehouse stash boxes.
2. Leaving Cash in Your Pockets: Dying with over $10,000 cash on hand deducts substantial medical/respawn penalties. Bank funds via Maze Bank app after every score.
3. Paying Impound Fees: Never pay the $1,500 police impound fee. Sneak into the impound lot at night, hop the back fence, and drive your car out for free.

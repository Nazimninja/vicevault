GTA 6 Radio Stations & Soundtrack — Leaked Songs & Artist Rumors

What songs are in GTA 6? Explore the leaked radio stations, artist tracklists, Florida-inspired stations, and dynamic music scores of Leonida.

Published: August 20, 2026 Author: Vice Vault Editorial Category: SOUNDTRACK Read time: undefined
08
SOUNDTRACK
RADIO BROADCAST INTEL

The music of Grand Theft Auto has always defined the atmosphere of its cities. For GTA 6, Rockstar is assembling a massive, diverse soundtrack that reflects the unique cultural melting pot of Florida. Let's analyze the radio stations and music systems.

Leonida Radio Station Culture

Vice City's radio waves will reflect a mix of classic 80s nostalgia alongside modern hip-hop, reggaeton, pop, synthwave, and country stations. Rockstar is reportedly creating specialized radio hosts and talk shows that parody Florida's viral news, social media influencers, and political climate, keeping the world feeling intensely satirical and realistic.

Expect return stations like Flash FM alongside brand-new stations dedicated to Latin beats and modern club music.

Leaked Soundtrack & Artist Rumors

Various leaks and artist teasers have suggested a star-studded tracklist. Rumored artists include prominent Florida-born musicians like Pitbull, Kodak Black, and Rick Ross, alongside pop superstars and classic rock legends. The debut trailer featured Tom Petty's 'Love Is A Long Road,' setting a strong classic rock tone for the rural counties of Leonida.

Soundtrack tip

Radio hosts will dynamically report on your story missions. If you pull off a major bank heist, listen to the news stations on the drive home to hear hosts debate the crime in real time.

Dynamic In-Car Radio System

GTA 6 introduces an interactive smartphone app that lets you stream radio stations even when walking or running, rather than being confined to car speakers. Additionally, the in-car audio system features advanced acoustic modeling—if you roll down the windows or open the convertible top, the radio music will disperse into the surrounding environment, blending with the city sounds.

Dynamic Action Scores

Similar to Red Dead Redemption 2, GTA 6 features a dynamic background score that plays during heists and high-stakes police chases. The tempo and instruments will shift dynamically based on combat intensity—when you are sneaking through a warehouse, the beat will be low and tense, but once gunshots are fired, the score transitions seamlessly into a fast-paced electronic or rock theme.
